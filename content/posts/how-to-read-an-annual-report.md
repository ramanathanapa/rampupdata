## 1. Snapshot vs movie

- 📸 **Balance sheet** = a snapshot on one day: what the company owns and owes
- 🎬 **Income statement** = a movie over a year: money earned minus money spent = profit

Your bank balance today is a balance sheet. Your salary minus spending this month is an income statement.

> 💡 Use the page number **printed** in the report, not your PDF viewer's.

*🎮 Chapter 1*

---

## 2. Read the scorecard first

The "key figures" page answers four questions in one glance:

| Question | DWS 2025 |
| --- | --- |
| More business coming in? | ✅ New money doubled to €51 bn |
| Profit growing? | ✅ Up \~40% to €1,324 m |
| More efficient? | ✅ Cost-income ratio down to 58% |
| Shareholders getting more? | ✅ Dividend up to €3.00 |

*🎮 Chapter 2*

---

## 3. EPS, dividend and P/E

Four friends own a shop making ₹40,000. Each friend's share is ₹10,000 (**EPS**). Each takes home ₹6,500 (**dividend**) and leaves the rest in the shop.

**P/E = share price ÷ EPS.** For DWS, €56.50 ÷ €4.64 ≈ **12**: about 12 years of today's profit per share.

*🎮 Chapter 3*

---

## 4. Can you trust it?

- Find the auditor's opinion. **"Unqualified" = clean.** DWS got one.
- Check the big owners. Deutsche Bank owns \~79% of DWS.
- Don't panic at routine news. DWS changing auditors is an EU rule after 10 years.

*🎮 Chapter 4*

---

## 5. Check old promises

Compare what management promised with what it delivered. DWS hit **3 of its 4** targets from 2022. A team that keeps promises earns trust for new ones.

> 📐 **CAGR** is steady yearly growth with compounding. ₹100 → ₹150 in 3 years is 14.5% a year, not 16.7%.

*🎮 Chapter 5*

---

## 6. Find the volume number

Every business has one number that drives everything: cars sold, subscribers, loans. For DWS it's **AUM**, the client money it manages (€1,085 bn).

⚠️ **AUM is clients' money, not DWS's.**

*🎮 Chapter 6*

---

## 7. How does it make money?

DWS earns a **fee**, like the expense ratio on an Indian mutual fund.

👉 Fee margin **0.25%** = about **€2.50 a year for every €1,000** managed.

Ask of any business: **volume × price**. Is each going up or down?

> 📏 1 basis point = 0.01%, so 25 bp = 0.25%.

*🎮 Chapter 7*

---

## 8. Follow €100 of revenue

For every **€100** DWS earned in 2025:

- **€58** → costs
- **€12.50** → tax
- **€29.50** → profit

The headline: revenue **+€390 m**, costs only **+€17 m**.

*🎮 Chapter 8*

---

## 9. Paper loss or real loss?

DWS made €928 m profit, yet its equity dipped. Why? A weaker dollar cut **€483 m** on paper. No cash left, and it reverses if the dollar recovers.

Also remember: **profit is not cash**. Check the cash flow statement too.

*🎮 Chapter 9*

---

## 10. Tell the story in one chain

```
Client money €1,085 bn
  × fee 0.25%       → fees ≈ €2.6 bn
  + other income    → revenue €3,155 m
  – costs, tax      → profit €928 m
  ÷ 200 m shares    → EPS €4.64
  → dividend €3.00
```

If you can write a chain like this, you understand the report.

*🎮 Chapter 10*

---

## ✅ Your 10-question checklist

1. Which is the snapshot and which is the movie?
2. Are business, profit, efficiency and payout up or down?
3. What are EPS, dividend and P/E?
4. Is the audit opinion clean? Who are the big owners?
5. Did management keep its promises?
6. What's the volume number, and why did it move?
7. How does it earn, and is its price rising or falling?
8. Out of ₹100 of revenue, how much is profit?
9. Which losses are real and which are paper?
10. Can I tell the story in one chain?
